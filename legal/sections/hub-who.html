Every app we publish has its own privacy policy and its own terms of use, because each one handles your information differently. There is no single "our app" that a combined policy could honestly describe — a drawing app that keeps everything on your device cannot share a page with a study app that sends a question to an AI provider, or with an app that stores an account on our servers.

So the rules are:

  • One policy per app. The policy covers what that app does, and nothing else. It is the URL you can give to Apple, to a school, or to a parent.
  • One publisher behind all of them. {{c.publisher}} is {{c.publisherKind}}, and it is the party responsible for every app listed above.
  • No data selling, anywhere. None of our apps sell your data, your work, or your content — that holds for every app on this page.
  • You can see what changed. Each policy shows its own effective date, so you can tell when an app's practice last changed.

If an app is marked draft above, its policy is not yet final and carries a noindex tag. Ask us before relying on it.
