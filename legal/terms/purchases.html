{{app.name}} offers optional paid features through the Apple App Store, either as a one-time purchase or as an auto-renewing subscription.

  • Payment is handled by Apple. We never see or store your card details. Apple charges your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase.
  • Auto-renewal. A subscription renews automatically for the same period unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your Apple Account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Cancellation. Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings ▸ Apple Account ▸ Subscriptions on your device. Cancelling stops the next renewal; it does not refund the current period.
  • Refunds are granted by Apple under Apple's policy, at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue App Store refunds ourselves.
  • Price changes. If we change a subscription price, Apple will notify you before the change applies to your renewal, and you may cancel.
