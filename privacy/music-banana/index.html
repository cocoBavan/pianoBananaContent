Overview & Core Privacy Vision

Welcome to Music Banana. Music Banana is an intelligent mobile application that recognises sheet music, transcribes the notes, and returns playable or structured music data directly on your device. It is published by HeyGhoshi, an independent Australian app studio.

This page is the privacy policy for Music Banana only. Our other apps publish their own policies, because each of them handles data differently — the legal index links to all of them. What follows describes exactly what this app does.

Our promise for Music Banana:

Your library is yours. Reading a page gives your notes back to you, and session recordings can be switched off at any time.

2

How Sheet Music Scanning Works

Music Banana reads a page of sheet music and turns it into playable notes and structured music data. Your scans are not used to train machine-learning models, and they are not added to a shared library.

  1. Image capture: you photograph a sheet music page with your device camera, or select an image with the system photo picker.
  2. Reading the page: the app detects staves, clefs, key signatures, and musical note values, using AI.
  3. What comes back: the recognised music is returned to you as notes, MIDI, MusicXML, or interactive playback.
  4. Your copy: the version you keep is the one you choose to save to your own library.
  5. Not for training: what you scan is never used to train machine-learning models, and it is never sold or published.

What we will not do with what you scan:

Your scans are not used to train models, not sold, and not published. Reading a page exists to give your notes back to you.

Where Your Data Lives

All of your saved items, music history, favourite scores, sound preferences, and offline transcriptions are stored on your personal device, not in an account of ours.

  • Your sheet music library remains in your device's isolated app sandbox storage.
  • If you back up your device through iCloud Backup or Android system backup, your local Music Banana data follows your system backup settings.
  • Deleting the Music Banana app permanently erases all locally stored sheet music unless you have exported your files first.

Device Permissions Requested

To provide its features, Music Banana may ask for the following device capabilities. Each one is requested by iOS at the moment it is first needed, and refusing any of them leaves the rest of the app working.

Camera

Required solely to photograph physical sheet music pages directly inside the app.

Photos

You can choose an image or PDF of a score that you have already saved. Music Banana uses the system photo picker, which hands the app only the file you select — it does not give us access to your photo library.

Tracking (optional)

iOS asks whether we may measure which ads brought you to Music Banana. Declining does not restrict any feature of the app.

Analytics & Advertising Measurement

To keep the app stable and to measure whether our advertising works, two third-party measurement services receive telemetry — usage events, and session recordings while they are switched on. A recording can include the sheet that is on screen. Sessions are described in full below.

Product analytics

Anonymous usage events (app opened, scan started, paywall shown, purchase completed) together with device model, operating-system version, app version, and locale. There are no accounts, so no event carries a name or email address, and none can be linked back to a particular score.

Session recordings

On by default until you answer the question, which the app asks at the end of the first-run walkthrough. You can change your answer at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy, where it appears as “Help Improve Music Banana”, and answering no changes nothing about the app. A recording is a series of screenshots of Music Banana's own screens, so it can include the sheet you are working on. It carries no name and no email address, and screens belonging to iOS or to other apps — the photo picker, share sheets, and the keyboard — are kept out of every recording.

Advertising measurement

App install, app launch, and in-app purchase events, so we can tell which campaigns bring people to Music Banana. If you allow tracking at the system prompt, these events also carry your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). If you decline, the events are still sent but without that identifier, which means we receive only aggregated, non-identifying counts.

* You can change your tracking choice at any time in iOS Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking. We do not sell your data, and we never send an advertising partner the content of anything you create in Music Banana.

Third-Party Service Providers

Apart from the two measurement services listed above, the only external service the app contacts is the content host that serves the built-in song library.

  • Measurement services — product analytics and session recordings, plus advertising measurement, as described above.
  • heyghoshi.com — our static content host, which serves the song catalogue and MusicXML files. These are downloads only: the request sends nothing about you or your device.
  • Apple — App Store distribution and in-app purchases, handled entirely by Apple.

We do not sell personal data. Beyond the services listed above, nothing about you is shared with anyone.

* Third-party services that handle your data publish their own privacy policies, and Apple — which handles App Store distribution and purchases — is one of them: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children's Privacy

Music Banana is rated for general audiences (Music & Education) and is not directed to children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children: there are no accounts, no profiles, and nowhere in the app to enter a name, email address, or other personal detail.

The advertising measurement described above applies to all users and is controlled by the system tracking prompt, which a parent or guardian can decline.

Your Data Rights & Deletion

Depending on what Music Banana keeps, your control works like this:

  • Clear the in-app library: you can delete any saved score inside Music Banana at any time.
  • Switch off recordings: turn them off in Settings ▸ Privacy and they stop immediately. If you would like recordings that have already been sent to be deleted, email us and we will remove them.
  • Reset app data: clearing the app storage or uninstalling Music Banana instantly and permanently deletes all data from your device.
  • No deletion request needed for your library: because there are no accounts, there is nothing of that kind to request deletion of — your scores are yours alone.

The one thing we do hold elsewhere is a session recording, and it carries no name and no email address. If you ask us to delete one, tell us roughly when the session happened and we will work with you to identify it.
