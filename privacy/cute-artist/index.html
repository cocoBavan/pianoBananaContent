Overview & Core Privacy Vision

Welcome to Cute Artist. Cute Artist is a drawing and colouring app with classic and magic brushes, animated characters, and background music that works on your device. It is published by HeyGhoshi, an independent Australian app studio.

This page is the privacy policy for Cute Artist only. Our other apps publish their own policies, because each of them handles data differently — the legal index links to all of them. What follows describes exactly what this app does.

Our promise for Cute Artist:

Your drawings stay on your device. No gallery, no upload, no account.

2

Where Your Data Lives

Your drawings, saved colourings, and preferences are created and kept on your device. We do not operate a gallery server and we do not upload your artwork anywhere.

  • Drawings are saved in the app's isolated sandbox storage on your device.
  • If you back up your device through iCloud Backup, your local Cute Artist data follows your system backup settings.
  • Deleting a saved colouring inside the app or deleting the Cute Artist app permanently erases your local drawings.
3

Analytics & Advertising Measurement

To keep the app stable and understand which features are used, a single analytics and diagnostics service receives limited, anonymous telemetry. It never receives your drawings, your canvas, or anything you create in the app.

Product analytics & diagnostics

Anonymous usage events (such as app opened, artwork opened, drawing started, drawing completed or saved, background music toggled, and purchase events) plus automatic error and crash diagnostics, together with device model, operating-system version, app version, and locale. There are no accounts, so no event carries a name or email address, and none can be linked to your drawings.

* You can change your tracking choice at any time in iOS Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking. We do not sell your data, and we never send an advertising partner the content of anything you create in Cute Artist.

4

Third-Party Service Providers

Apart from the analytics and diagnostics service listed above, the app only connects to our static content host to download artwork packs and background music, and to Apple for App Store purchases.

  • Analytics & diagnostics service — anonymous usage events and crash/error logs as described above.
  • Static content host — serves downloadable artwork packs and background music tracks. These are downloads only: the request sends nothing about you or your drawings.
  • Apple — App Store distribution, in-app purchases, and purchase restoration, handled entirely by Apple.

We do not sell personal data. Beyond the services listed above, nothing about you is shared with anyone.

* Third-party services that handle your data publish their own privacy policies, and Apple — which handles App Store distribution and purchases — is one of them: apple.com/legal/privacy.

5

Children's Privacy

Cute Artist is rated for general audiences (Entertainment & Art) and is not directed to children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children: there are no accounts, no profiles, and nowhere in the app to enter a name, email address, or other personal detail.

Cute Artist is made to be enjoyed by young artists, and we have deliberately kept it free of accounts, social feeds, uploads, user-generated content sharing, and advertising tracking.

6

Your Data Rights & Deletion

Depending on what Cute Artist keeps, your control works like this:

  • Clear your saved colourings: you can delete any saved drawing inside Cute Artist at any time.
  • Reset app data: clearing the app storage or uninstalling Cute Artist instantly and permanently deletes all local drawings and settings from your device.
  • No deletion request needed: because we do not store accounts or artwork on cloud servers, there is no server-side copy to request deletion of.

Nothing to export or request from us, because we hold no personal data or artwork about you.
