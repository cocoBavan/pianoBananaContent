General App Store & Subscription FAQ

Common questions across all HeyGhoshi applications.

How do I restore purchases across devices?
All purchases are linked to your Apple ID. Open the app, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID used to make the purchase.
How do I manage or cancel an active subscription?
Subscriptions are managed through Apple: Open your iOS device's Settings app > tap your Apple ID name > tap Subscriptions > choose the app and tap Cancel Subscription.
What are the system requirements?
Our apps are designed for iOS 17.0 and iPadOS 17.0 or later, supporting iPhone and iPad models.
