Selective Teacher Support & User Guide

Shows the working, not just the answer. Visual AI tutoring for primary school exam prep.

Selective Teacher helps primary school students, parents, and educators master challenging questions in Mathematics, English, and Thinking Skills. Designed around primary school curricula and competitive scholarship exams (such as Selective High School placement tests, Opportunity Class tests, and 11+ exams), the app turns tricky problems into step-by-step visual lessons with voiceover narration and unlimited adaptive practice.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

How to Use Selective Teacher

Master problem scanning, interactive visual lessons, adaptive practice quizzes, and error diagnostics.

1

Snap, Select, or Type Any Problem

Input any homework or exam question in seconds using your iPhone or iPad camera, choosing a saved image from your photo library, or typing text directly into the problem field.

  1. 1 Open the app and tap the Camera icon to photograph your worksheet, or tap Photo Library for an existing scan or screenshot.
  2. 2 Use the interactive Crop Frame to isolate just the specific question your child is stuck on, removing surrounding questions and distracting margins.
  3. 3 Our optical recognition parses the text, numbers, formulas, and diagrams automatically, allowing quick text edits before generating the explanation.
  4. 4 Alternatively, tap the Text Input bar to type or paste word problems directly.
Pro-Tips for Crisp Recognition:
  • Crop tightly: Focus on one problem at a time so the tutor provides targeted, uncluttered solutions.
  • Direct lighting: Avoid casting dark phone shadows across formulas or diagrams.
  • Handwritten notes: Ensure handwriting is legible; printed textbook pages work with near 100% accuracy.
Capture Checklist
✓ Question text & options clearly visible
✓ Accompanying table/diagram included
✓ No harsh glare or bent paper creases
✓ Surrounding unrelated questions cropped out

4

Practice with Adaptive Variations

Understanding one solution is good; being able to solve that problem when the numbers or wording change is mastery.

🎯

"Practice More Like This"

At the conclusion of any explanation, tap Practice More. The app generates brand-new question variations preserving the core logic pattern.

This prevents rote memorization of specific numbers and tests genuine comprehension under simulated exam conditions.

🔄

Adaptive Feedback & Hints

If your child gets a practice question wrong, the app doesn't just reveal the answer. It highlights where the misstep occurred and offers immediate visual hints.

Students can generate unlimited variation quizzes until they achieve a 100% mastery score.

5

Quiz Diagnostic Analysis & Region Settings

Go beyond basic test percentages with intelligent root-cause diagnosis, localized curriculum standards, and parental controls.

🔬

Carelessness vs. Concept Gaps

After completing a quiz, tap Analyze My Performance. Our AI categorizes errors into:

  • Careless Mistakes: Misread signs or arithmetic slips (flagged for review without repetitive retraining).
  • Understanding Gaps: Missing core concepts, providing a 1-tap button to launch a dedicated visual teaching scene.

Help & Troubleshooting

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about problem scanning, subjects, subscriptions, and parent controls.

What exam formats and year levels does Selective Teacher support?
Selective Teacher is primarily tailored for primary school students in Years 3 to 6 preparing for competitive entrance tests—including the NSW Selective High School Placement Test, Opportunity Class (OC) placement, Victorian Select Entry, Queensland Academic Select Entry, ACER & Edutest scholarship exams, UK 11+ Grammar exams, and general primary school homework across Math, English, and Thinking Skills.
Can the scanner read handwritten questions as well as printed worksheets?
Yes. Our OCR model handles both printed textbook typography and legible student handwriting. For optimal accuracy, crop out any scribbles, ensure good lighting without dark shadows, and review the recognized text preview before submitting. You can make manual corrections directly in the text field if any symbol is misread.
What are Thinking Skills and how does the app teach them?
Thinking Skills (which replaced General Ability in many competitive exams) covers critical thinking, syllogistic reasoning, pattern deduction, and spatial relationships. Rather than offering abstract text explanations, our Thinking Skills tutor renders visual Venn diagrams, truth tables, and step-by-step logic matrices that make abstract deduction immediately intuitive.
How do I change currency, measurement units, or curriculum spelling?
Go to Settings > Region. You can choose between Australia, the United States, the United Kingdom, India, Canada, and New Zealand. All newly generated practice questions, currency word problems (e.g. $, £, or ₹), and measurement units (metric vs. imperial) will conform to your chosen country.
How does Parent PIN Protection work?
To prevent young children from modifying subscription settings or triggering unnecessary generations, navigate to Settings > PIN & Security and create a 4-digit security PIN. Once enabled, parents can lock settings and access administrative controls securely.
What is the difference between the Free plan and Pro (Premium)?
  • • Free Plan: Includes starter question scans, access to sample explanations across all subjects, and standard text explanations.
  • • Pro Plan: Unlocks unlimited question generations, full access to animated visual diagrams across Math, English, and Thinking Skills, unlimited adaptive practice quiz sets, full audio narration, and deep diagnostic error analysis. Both Monthly and Annual billing options include a free introductory trial.
How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
If you upgrade your iPhone or iPad, open Selective Teacher, go to Settings > Premium, and tap Restore Purchases. StoreKit 2 will automatically synchronize your active subscription with the Apple ID signed in on your device.
How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
All subscriptions and free trials are managed safely and securely by Apple:
  1. Open the iOS Settings app on your device.
  2. Tap your Name / Apple ID banner at the top.
  3. Select Subscriptions.
  4. Tap Selective Teacher and choose Cancel Subscription. You retain full Pro access until the end of the current billing cycle or trial period.
