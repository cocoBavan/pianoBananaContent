Cute Artist Support & User Guide

Colour step by step, then watch your creation come to life.

Cute Artist is a playful drawing and colouring app designed for young artists on iPhone and iPad. Pick a character or scene, colour each part with classic and magic brushes, and enjoy cheerful animations and sound effects when you finish—with all your saved drawings kept privately on your device.

Guided Step-by-Step

Colour each part of a character or scene one step at a time with helpful outlines so every drawing looks great.

Classic & Magic Brushes

Switch between Crayon, Marker, Watercolor, Pencil, Rainbow, Neon, Glitter, and Polka Dots.

Living Animations

When you finish colouring, your artwork animates on screen with playful sound effects.

Private & Local

No accounts, no public gallery, and no ad tracking. All saved drawings stay on your device.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

How to Create & Save in Cute Artist

From picking a character to watching your finished drawing animate.

1

Choose an Artwork or Revisit a Saved Drawing

Browse the artwork gallery to pick a character or scene you want to colour. If you have already saved drawings, you can swipe through your saved collection to view them or replay their animations anytime.

Free & Premium Artworks

Starter artworks are ready to colour right away. Additional characters and scenes unlock with Cute Artist Premium.

Saved Carousel

Every drawing you save appears in your local saved carousel so you can admire or delete it whenever you like.

2

Colour with Classic & Magic Brushes

Tap any crayon along the side to pick a colour family, then choose a shade from the shade palette. Pick your brush style and brush size, and draw freely with your finger or Apple Pencil.

Classic Brushes

Crayon, Marker, Watercolor, and Pencil give natural textures and smooth strokes, plus an Eraser to tidy up.

Magic Brushes

Add playful flair with Rainbow, Neon, Glitter, and Polka Dots (with custom dot pattern styles).

3

Watch Your Artwork Animate & Save It

Use the Next and Previous controls to move through each part of the drawing. When the final part is complete, your finished character comes to life on screen with animation and sound effects.

Save Locally

Tap Save to store your finished colouring on your device so you can revisit it anytime.

Delete Anytime

Want to start fresh or tidy up? Tap Delete on any saved colouring to remove it immediately from your device.

4

Settings, Music & Cute Artist Premium

Open Settings from the home screen to customise background music or manage your Cute Artist Premium access.

Background Music

Toggle Background Music on or off at any time to play or mute cheerful music while you draw.

Premium & Restore Purchases

Unlock all artwork and brushes with a Monthly subscription or a one-time Lifetime unlock. Use Restore Purchases to sync your Apple ID entitlement on any device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions & Troubleshooting

Where are my saved drawings stored?
All drawings and saved colourings are stored locally on your iPhone or iPad inside the app's private storage. Cute Artist does not have user accounts or an online gallery, and your artwork is never uploaded to our servers.
Does Cute Artist work offline?
Yes—once an artwork pack is downloaded (or bundled in the app), you can colour and view your saved drawings offline. An internet connection is only needed to download new artwork packs or background music tracks, or to make or restore App Store purchases.
How do I turn off background music?
Open Settings inside Cute Artist and switch Background Music off. Your preference is saved automatically.
How do I restore my purchases on another device?
All purchases are linked to your Apple ID. Open Cute Artist, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID used to make the original purchase.
How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
All subscriptions are billed securely through Apple and can be managed anytime in iOS Settings:
  1. Open your iOS device's Settings app.
  2. Tap your Apple ID profile name at the very top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Select Cute Artist and choose Cancel Subscription.
How do I delete my data?
Because Cute Artist has no accounts and stores all drawings locally on your device, you can delete individual saved colourings inside the app or uninstall Cute Artist to permanently erase all local drawings and settings.
