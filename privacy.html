Overview & Core Privacy Vision

Welcome to Piano Banana ("we", "our", or "us"). Piano Banana is an intelligent mobile application designed to assist musicians by recognizing sheet music, transcribing musical notes, and returning playable or structured music data directly back to your device.

We built Piano Banana around a core principle: Your creative work is private. Unlike traditional cloud applications that archive your uploads in distant database clusters, Piano Banana acts as an immediate processing pipeline without persisting any of your content on server storage.

2

How Sheet Music Scanning Works

When you capture or upload a photograph of a music score inside Piano Banana, the following sequence occurs:

  1. Image Capture: You photograph a sheet music page using your device camera or select a photo from your local media library.
  2. Secure Transmission: The image is encrypted using industry-standard SSL/TLS (HTTPS) during transit and dispatched to our processing endpoint.
  3. AI Analysis: The AI engine inspects the image solely to detect staves, clefs, key signatures, and musical note values.
  4. Data Return: The extracted musical output (such as MIDI, XML, or interactive playback formats) is returned directly back to your application.
  5. Immediate Purge: Once the server finishes generating the musical response, the source image and transient files are permanently deleted from server RAM/volatile memory.
3

AI Engine & Zero Server Retention

We guarantee that nothing from your scans is stored on server-side disks or used to train public machine learning models.

Our Pass-Through Guarantee:

Server memory holds image data strictly during the millisecond runtime required to calculate musical geometry. No database entries, persistent logs, or cloud backups of your music sheets are ever created.

4

On-Device Local Storage

Because our servers store zero user data, 100% of your saved items, music history, favorite scores, sound preferences, and offline transcriptions are stored directly on your personal device.

  • Your sheet music library remains in your device's isolated app sandbox storage.
  • If you back up your device via iOS iCloud Backup or Android System Backup, your local Piano Banana data follows your system backup settings.
  • Deleting the Piano Banana app from your device will permanently erase all locally stored sheet music unless you have exported your files externally.
5

Device Permissions Requested

To provide core features, Piano Banana requests access to specific hardware capabilities:

Camera Permission

Required solely to photograph physical sheet music pages directly within the application.

Photo Library Permission

Allows you to select previously saved images or PDFs of musical scores for AI processing.

Microphone / Audio Output (Optional)

Used for interactive pitch playback or tuning utilities. We do not record or transmit ambient background conversations.

6

Analytics & Diagnostics

To maintain app stability and improve recognition speeds, we may collect minimal, aggregated telemetry data:

  • Anonymized crash reports (e.g., call stack traces, device model, operating system version).
  • Aggregated performance metrics (e.g., average time taken for AI sheet transcription).

* Note: Telemetry data contains no personally identifiable information (PII) and can never be linked back to individual sheet music scans.

7

Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with secure cloud infrastructure providers (such as Amazon Web Services / Google Cloud / OpenAI API) exclusively for real-time computational inference.

All third-party infrastructure partners are contractually bound under Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) strictly forbidding them from storing, mining, or sharing any transient payload data processed on behalf of Piano Banana users.

8

Children's Privacy

Piano Banana is suitable for music students of all ages, including young children learning piano. Because we do not collect accounts, personal profiles, real names, or persistent images, we fully comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR child privacy standards.

9

Your Data Rights & Deletion

Since all data resides on your phone, you have absolute control over your information at all times:

  • Clear In-App Library: You can delete any saved sheet music score inside Piano Banana at any time.
  • Reset App Data: Clearing the app storage or uninstalling Piano Banana instantly and permanently deletes all data from your device.
  • No Account Deletion Request Needed: Because we don't store accounts on cloud servers, you don't need to request server-side deletion—your data is already yours alone.
